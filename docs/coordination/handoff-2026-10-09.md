## 1. What the project is

**SpectraQuant: Budget-Aware Joint Low-Rank and Low-Bit Training for Efficient Transformers.**
Research question: does preparing a transformer for low-rank factorization improve or damage its
subsequent quantization, and can an output-aware sensitivity proxy pick layer-wise ranks and bit
widths that beat uniform compression on a quality–memory frontier?

Governing documents: `AGENTS.md` (binding contract), `docs/research/preregistration.md` (**frozen**
2026-10-09 at `6281c56`), `docs/research/preregistration-amendments.md` (A-0001 … A-0012).

## 3. What has been achieved, by milestone

### M0 — bootstrap (**PASSED**)
Repo, `AGENTS.md`, layout, CI, uv lock, CLI. Gate: a fresh clone of the pushed branch passes
`uv sync --all-extras`, lint, type check, unit tests, and `spectraquant smoke` reproduces the same
loss-sequence digest (`sha256:1b2e0622…`) as the working tree.

### M1 — literature, novelty, freeze (**PASSED, narrowed scope**)
Literature matrix, novelty audit with an independent adversarial review
(`docs/results/verification/m1-novelty-review.md`): verdicts A *differentiated (narrow, empirical)*,
B *differentiated (weak–moderate)*, C *previously known as a concept*; 17 blocking issues, all closed
(two uncited neighbours found — SVDQuant, JoLT; H2 unit redesigned with a predeclared MDE). Upstreams
pinned in `docs/research/upstream-lockfile.md`.

### M2 — math and allocator fixtures (**PASSED for implemented slices**)
Exact float64 agreement with hand-computed toys (`rtol=1e-9`); `accounted_bytes == measured` with zero
residual across 16 configurations; CP-SAT allocator equals the exhaustive oracle at three budgets;
no-CUDA invariant test; equal-memory gate exercised by a negative test.

### M3 — bounded reproduction of LR-QAT / LoftQ (**IN PROGRESS — one negative cell, one re-run pending**)
See §4. The first GPU cells ran; the result is negative and attributable, and the cause was a defect
in our own corpus sizing, now fixed. **No reproduction claim is made in either direction yet.**

### M4 — proxy validation (**LOCAL-FIXTURE HALF PASSED; CLOUD HALF NOT RUN**)
On the Tier-0 fixture (5 seeds × 6 (rank, bits) cells × 2 fixtures) the predeclared candidate
`gain_aware_composed` has a positive Fisher-z advantage over every predeclared comparator in **15/15**
aggregate contrasts, CI excluding 0; achieved MDE 0.476–0.715 (inside the predeclared 0.33–0.79
band). The candidate's *own* contrast MDE is 0.968 pooled — wider than the conservative end, so the
fixture is a coarse instrument for that contrast. The confirmatory H2/H4 cells are Tier-1 and
**NOT RUN**.

### M5 — the method (**NOT MET on the fixture — honest negative**)
The regularizer reduces the rounding residual and improves fidelity to the dense reference but
*worsens* dev NLL: the two quality axes disagree in sign, so H3 is unsupported locally (A-0008). The
confirmatory H3 cell is Tier-1 and **NOT RUN**.

### M6 — allocation accounting (**PASSED on the fixture**)
18/18 arms reconcile class-3 measured bytes with class-1 accounting inside the 0.5 % tolerance;
manifests replay deterministically; the equal-memory gate refuses byte-unequal pairs.

### M7 — cloud execution (**PIPELINE COMPLETE; TWO RUNS VALIDATED**)
`generate → submit → poll → fetch → checksum-validate → registry` works end to end on Kaggle, with
fail-loud behaviour instead of silent fallbacks. Two runs are in the registry as `validated` (§4).

### Class 4-CPU (**AVAILABLE and used**)
An int4 ONNX artifact we serialize ourselves executes through ONNX Runtime's `MatMulNBits` CPU kernel
(payload 1024 B + scales 256 B inside a 1501 B artifact); int8 through `MatMulInteger` (2627 B).
Relative error `0.0771`; **no speedup claimed**. This makes H5 locally testable in the CPU scope.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fp16_reference` | 14.0181 | 212 336 640 | 1.00× | — |
| `ptq_uniform_8` (per-group g=32) | 14.0412 (+0.17 %) | 112 803 840 | 1.88× | 2 |
| `ptq_uniform_4` (per-group g=32) | 18.6190 (+32.8 %) | 59 719 680 | 3.56× | 2 |

### 4.2 M3 reproduction cells — `repro_lr_qat_loftq_smollm2_135m-cloud` (**validated**, Tesla T4)
Two collected bundles. Full record: `docs/results/m3-reproduction-2026-10-09.md`.

| arm | perplexity **at init** | perplexity **after training** | bytes | class | train wall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fp16_reference` | — | 14.0183 | 212 336 640 | — | — |
| `lr_qat` (rank 8, 4-bit) | **17.722** | 43.0962 | 64 604 160 | 2 | 1062 s |
| `loftq` (rank 8, 4-bit, T=3) | **17.793** | 269.6186 | 64 604 160 | 2 | 1072 s |
| `rank_then_quant` (rank 8, 4-bit factors) | — | degenerate 5.0e19 | 1 598 400 | 2 | — |

Dev-loss trajectory (per 100 steps): `lr_qat` 3.3168 → 3.4786 → 3.6982 → 3.9962 → 4.3478 (task loss
0.466); `loftq` 3.5124 → 4.3353 → 4.9668 → 5.6575 → 6.0777 (task loss 0.063).

**Reading:** both initialisations are sound (≈17.7 against 14.0183 fp16 = a 26 % cost for rank-8 +
4-bit). Training destroyed both arms because the corpus was **99 windows** while 500 steps at batch 8
drew 4 000 sequences from it — memorisation (task loss 0.06) with dev loss rising monotonically. The
corpus size was on the command line while the step count was in the plan; that is now fixed
(`TrainingSchedule.corpus_documents`). A re-run with 300 steps over 5 000 streamed documents (≈1
epoch) is in flight at the time of writing.

**Measured cost:** **2.17 s per optimiser step** at batch 8 × seq 512 on a T4 (433 s / 200 steps) —
this is the measurement the design note asked for before pinning a schedule.

## 5. What the cloud phase exposed (10 defects, all fixed, all now regression-tested)

None were visible to the 1034-test suite, because it drives a fake runner. The lesson is recorded:
**the fake-runner suite proves the protocol, not the platform contract.**

Platform/notebook: import-before-install; the `models` extra never installed; a syntax error in a
generated cell; a runner failure recorded with no reason; the teardown cell referencing names the
stages own; the plans pinning the dataset repository and revision but not its **config**.

Collection/registry: the registry stored the **redacted** remote id (so every later platform call was
denied); the collector picked a nested per-arm manifest and rejected a valid bundle; `--bits`
silently overrode the width in an arm's name (two arms produced identical numbers); a degenerate
perplexity recorded as if it were a measurement; a run could not be resubmitted from
`validated`/`rejected`/`failed`/`collected`.

GPU enablement: `uv run` re-syncing CPU torch over the CUDA install; no verification that the CUDA
build was the installed one; `==2.14.1` matching `2.14.1+cpu` under PEP 440 (silent no-op install);
no 2.14.1 on the cu128 index at all; `load_model` never moving the model to the device; CPU-only
primitives receiving CUDA tensors; the train corpus materialising C4 (hundreds of GB) instead of
streaming; a trainable arm's replaced layers leaking into the next arm.

Also fixed from review: **the LoftQ pairing**. The primitive returns the low-rank half only and its
companion base is `fake_quantize(w − B@A)`; pairing the factors with `fake_quantize(w)` gave 75 %
reconstruction error against 7.3 % for the naive baseline. Measured after the fix (64×48 weight,
int4/group-32/rank-8): base alone 9.86 %, one-step residual SVD 7.29 %, LoftQ T=1 6.18 %, T=3 5.71 %,
T=5 5.56 % — monotone in T, the published trend.

## 7. Open questions for you (the ones I would want an opinion on)

1. **Tier-1 training budget.** 2.17 s/step is measured. A 5 000-document corpus at 300 steps is ≈1
   epoch. Do you want the reproduction cell to run longer (more steps/epochs, more seeds), given the
   free tier is ~30 GPU-hours per week and a full 4-arm run costs ~50 min including install?
2. **Corpus choice.** The plans pin C4 `en` as the train/calibration corpus, streamed. C4 is huge and
   noisy; a smaller, cleaner, equally licensed corpus (e.g. a fixed SlimPajama or WikiText-103 slice)
   might make Tier-1 training more stable and cheaper to reproduce. This is a preregistration
   amendment either way.
3. **The M3 verdict rule.** If training cannot beat the initialisation at Tier-1 scale, is that a
   *failed reproduction* (report as such and move to M5), or a *scale finding* (LR-QAT/LoftQ need a
   larger model/corpus to show their trend)? I have been treating it as the former.
4. **Priority order for the next phase.** My default would be: finish M3 → M5 (the method) → M4 cloud
   (proxy on the real model). If you would rather see the proxy cell first (it is the H2 confirmatory
   cell and needs no training), say so.
