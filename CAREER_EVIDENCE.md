# CAREER_EVIDENCE.md — claim ledger

One row per potential CV/résumé statement. Every row maps a claim to the exact evidence that
supports it: the supporting commit, the machine-readable artifact, the generated table/figure, and a
public URL. **A bracketed `[PLACEHOLDER]` means the evidence does not exist yet.**

> **No placeholder may enter a CV.** A statement is CV-legal only when its status is **measured**,
> **independently reproduced** or **publicly released**, its artifact path exists in the repository,
> and its numbers appear verbatim in that artifact. Rows marked **planned** or **implemented** are
> not CV-legal claims about results: implement (not measured) may describe tooling, but it must not
> be phrased as a finding; planned is not CV-legal at all. Negative results are CV-legal and must be
> stated as negatives.

Status vocabulary: **planned** · **implemented** · **measured** · **independently reproduced** ·
**publicly released**. Measurement classes are `AGENTS.md` §5; substrate labels are `AGENTS.md` §2b.

Public base URL: `https://github.com/OussemaHarrabi/SpectraQuant` (branch `infra/bootstrap`). Commits
listed below are on that branch; a path becomes fetchable at
`…/blob/infra/bootstrap/<path>` once the branch is pushed. Rows whose commit is not yet pushed say so.

## 2. Permitted statement templates (bracketed values unfilled)

The master orchestrator specification is an external prompt (delivered 2026-10-08; only its distilled
charter is committed at `docs/research/charter.md`). Its permitted per-statement forms are reproduced
below with **every bracketed value left unfilled**. A template may be instantiated **only** from a
ledger row whose status is measured / independently reproduced / publicly released, and every filled
bracket must be copied verbatim from that row's artifact.

1. **Measured (positive).**
   `Implemented [COMPONENT] and measured [METRIC] = [VALUE] ([MEASUREMENT-CLASS]) on a [TIER/SUBSTRATE]
   configuration; evidence: [ARTIFACT-PATH], regenerated as [TABLE/FIGURE].`

2. **Measured (negative).**
   `Reported the negative result that [METHOD] [FAILURE-MODE] on a [TIER/SUBSTRATE] configuration
   ([VALUE] vs [BASELINE]; evidence: [ARTIFACT-PATH]).`

3. **Independently reproduced.**
   `[COMPONENT] was independently reproduced: [SECOND-PARTY] re-ran [SCRIPT] and reproduced
   [QUANTITY] ([REFERENCE-PATH]).`

4. **Implemented (tooling, no result).**
   `Built [TOOLING] with [GUARANTEE], verified by [TEST-PATH]; no scientific claim is made from it.`

5. **Publicly released.**
   `Released [ARTIFACT/PACKAGE] at [PUBLIC-URL] under [LICENSE], reproducible from [LOCKFILE/COMMIT].`

6. **Protocol / methodology.**
   `Pre-registered [PROTOCOL] with [PREDECLARED-CONSTRAINTS] before any confirmatory run; frozen at
   [COMMIT] ([DOCUMENT-PATH]).`

7. **Planned (NOT CV-legal until the evidence exists).**
   `Will evaluate [METHOD] on [MODEL/DATASET] under [PROTOCOL]; not yet run.` — a planned row is not a
   CV statement and must be dropped if a deadline arrives before the evidence does.

**Rule.** Fill the brackets from the ledger; never from memory, a console, or a paper's abstract. If a
bracket cannot be filled from an artifact path that exists, the row is not CV-legal.
